## Brand

- **English**: Soulync
- **Chinese**: 灵犀
- **Tagline EN**: Meet in 48 hours. Not 48 messages.
- **Tagline ZH**: 48小时内见面，而不是48条消息。

## AI Persona: Cupid 丘比特

The AI is NOT a generic assistant. It is **Cupid** — a warm, witty, emotionally intelligent wingman/bestie.

Personality traits:
- Talks like your smart best friend, not a therapist or a robot
- Uses casual, warm language — "I gotta say, that answer tells me a lot about you"
- Gently pushes users out of their comfort zone
- Celebrates small wins — "Look at you being vulnerable! Love that."
- Never uses phrases like "AI recommends" or "system suggests"
- All communications come from "Cupid" — first person, with personality
- Chinese persona: equally warm, uses 你 not 您, casual like a close friend

Examples:
- Instead of "AI recommends this venue" → "I picked this spot for you two — trust me on this one"
- Instead of "AI analysis complete" → "OK I think I've figured you out 😏 Ready to see your profile?"
- Instead of "Match found" → "I found someone. And honestly? I'm kind of excited about this one."

## Complete User Flow

### Phase 1: Cupid Gets to Know You (Attached Theory)

**Round 1: Icebreaker (4-5 questions)**
- Cupid introduces itself: "Hey! I'm Cupid, your personal matchmaker. I'm going to ask you some questions — not the boring 'what's your favorite color' kind. The real stuff. Ready?"
- Questions probe: openness, emotional reactions, core values, lifestyle
- Text input (voice input: greyed out mic button, "coming soon")

**Round 2: Going Deeper (4-5 questions)**
- Cupid says: "OK you passed round 1. Now I need to go deeper — don't worry, I'll be gentle."
- Questions probe: attachment style, conflict patterns, intimacy needs, vulnerability
- Behavioral inference, not self-report

**Output: Soul Profile**
- NOT just percentages and labels
- A CHARACTER ARCHETYPE, like MBTI does:
  - e.g. "The Lighthouse 灯塔" — "In relationships, you're the steady one. You give people direction and safety. But sometimes you forget that lighthouses need maintenance too."
  - e.g. "The Explorer 探索者" — "You crave depth but you also need space to breathe. You fall hard but you need a partner who won't try to clip your wings."
- Archetype name + 2-3 sentence poetic description + practical insight
- Attachment style, Big Five bars, love language shown as SECONDARY details below the archetype
- Bilingual: both EN and ZH versions

### Phase 2: Cupid Finds Your Match (Attached Algorithm)

**Matching rules (from "Attached" by Amir Levine):**
- NEVER match anxious + avoidant (the trap)
- Secure + any = good
- Secure + anxious = great (secure calms anxiety)
- Secure + avoidant = great (secure gives space)
- Values alignment weighted heavily
- Personality complementarity (some difference is good)

**Match presentation:**
- Cupid announces: "I found someone. And honestly? I'm kind of excited about this one."
- Compatibility score (0-100%)
- WHY they match (2-3 sentences, specific to both profiles) — KEEP THIS, Alex liked it
- Growth areas (1 sentence about potential friction)
- Full profile locked until after first date

### Phase 3: Cupid Arranges the Date

**3 venue options generated by AI:**
- Based on both users' preferences, locations, personality types
- Public venues only (safety requirement)
- Within reasonable travel distance for both

**Double-selection process:**
1. Both users independently pick their preferred venue
2. If same choice → date locked
3. If different → Cupid generates 3 NEW options, second round
4. If still different → Cupid picks the best compromise and asks both to confirm

**Restaurant booking responsibility:**
- Heterosexual match: male user books the restaurant
- Same-sex match: the user with higher attachment security score books
- Cupid tells the booking user: "Hey, I need you to book [venue]. Once you've got a reservation, share the confirmation with me and I'll let [partner name] know. Make it happen! 💪"
- Cupid forwards reservation confirmation to the other user

**Confirmation + commitment mechanism:**
- Once date is locked, Cupid sends: "This is happening. [Date], [Time], [Venue]. Your secret phrase is: '[phrase]'. If you can't make it, let me know at least 3 hours before. No-shows get a 3-day timeout. Don't be that person."
- Secret phrase: fun, memorable phrase both users say when they meet — KEEP THIS

**Reminder system:**
- 24 hours before: "Tomorrow's the day! Remember, [venue] at [time]."
- 3 hours before: "3 hours until your date. Last chance to reschedule without penalty. Everything good?"
- 1 hour before: "Almost time! Take a deep breath. You've got this."

**Cancellation rules:**
- Cancel 3+ hours before: no penalty, date rescheduled
- Cancel <3 hours before: 3-day app suspension
- No-show: 3-day app suspension
- Cupid message for cancellation: "No worries, life happens. I'll let [partner] know and we'll reschedule when you're ready."

### Phase 4: The Date — 12 Questions (Arthur Aron Protocol)

**Pre-date:**
- Cupid sends both users a "date card" with the evening's format
- "Tonight's plan: 12 questions, 2 minutes of eye contact, and maybe the start of something real."

**Question flow:**
- 4 LIGHT questions (build comfort, ~10 min)
- 4 MEDIUM questions (build trust, ~10 min)  
- 4 DEEP questions (build intimacy, ~10 min)
- Questions are PERSONALIZED by Cupid based on both profiles
  - Anxious + Secure pair: questions that let secure person demonstrate reliability
  - Avoidant + Secure pair: questions that respect autonomy, gradual vulnerability
- Both users open the app, questions appear one at a time
- They answer OUT LOUD to each other, not in the app
- Tap "next" when both have answered

**Eye contact exercise:**
- Duration: 2 MINUTES (not 4 — first date, don't overdo it)
- After 2 minutes: "That was beautiful. Want to go another 2 minutes?" (optional extension)
- Cupid instruction: "Put your phones face-down. Look at each other. No talking. Just... be there. I'll tell you when time's up."

### Phase 5: After the Date — Feedback Before Unlock

**This is critical: chat does NOT unlock immediately.**

Flow:
1. Date ends → both users get Cupid message: "So... how was it? 😊"
2. Cupid asks each user privately (the other person cannot see):
   - "How would you rate tonight?" (1-5 stars across dimensions)
   - Attraction / Conversation quality / Values alignment / Emotional safety
   - "Would you want to see [name] again?" (Yes / Not sure / No)
3. Results:
   - **Both say Yes** → Chat unlocked. Cupid says: "You both want to see each other again! 🎉 Chat's open. Here's something to talk about..." + personalized icebreaker based on date experience
   - **One says Yes, one says Not Sure** → Chat unlocked but Cupid manages expectations: "Chat's open! Take it at your own pace."
   - **One or both say No** → Chat stays locked.
     - To the rejected: Cupid gives warm, specific encouragement. NOT "there are other fish in the sea." Instead: "I know this one stings. But here's what I noticed about you tonight — [specific positive observation]. The right person will appreciate that. I'm already looking for your next match."
     - To the rejecter: "Thanks for being honest. That takes courage. I'll keep this between us."

**Cupid's post-date insights (if mutual Yes):**
- Shares small details to deepen connection: "Fun fact: [name] mentioned loving [specific thing from their profile] — you two should definitely check out [related suggestion]"
- Suggests second date ideas based on what went well
- Gives relationship-specific advice based on their attachment combo

### Phase 6: Cupid as Ongoing Companion

**MVP scope (user-initiated only, no push notifications):**
- Users can open a "Chat with Cupid" screen anytime
- Ask relationship questions: "Is it normal that she takes 3 hours to reply?"
- Get advice: "What should I plan for our second date?"
- Process feelings: "I'm not sure if I like him or if I'm just lonely"
- Cupid responds with warmth, referencing their psych profile for personalized advice

**Post-MVP (when native app is built):**
- Daily evening check-in: "Hey, how are things with [partner name]? Anything on your mind?"
- Milestone celebrations: "You two have been chatting for a week! 🎉"
- Gentle nudges: "Haven't heard from you in a while. Everything okay?"

## Safety Features

- All date venues must be public places (Cupid filters for this)
- Emergency contact: one-tap share current location with trusted contact
- Report user function
- 3-hour cancellation window without penalty
- 3-day suspension for no-shows
- All conversations encrypted

## Key Product Decisions Log

| Decision | Choice | Reason |
|----------|--------|--------|
| AI persona | Cupid 丘比特 | More human, users open up more |
| Match mode | AI direct assignment | Black Mirror style, no swiping |
| Pre-date | 12 personalized questions | Arthur Aron protocol, adapted |
| Eye contact | 2 min + optional 2 min | 4 min too long for first date |
| Chat unlock | After mutual feedback | Creates intentionality |
| No-show penalty | 3-day suspension | Enough deterrent, not too harsh |
| Restaurant booking | Male books (hetero) / higher security books (same-sex) | Cultural norm + psychological fit |
| Cancel window | 3 hours before | Fair, with automated notifications |
| Voice input | Post-MVP | Too complex for 10-day timeline |
| Push notifications | Post-MVP | Requires native app |
| Target market | Global (EN + ZH bilingual) | Dual market from day 1 |
| AI model | DeepSeek V4 | Cheapest, bilingual, OpenAI-compatible |
